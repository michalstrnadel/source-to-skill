# 07: Superintelligence, loss of control, and why AI blends into 2% growth (1:17:36–1:32:50)

Chapter "ASI". This is also where the "AGI will blend into 2% GDP growth" debate
takes place.

## What superintelligence looks like

- It is **a progression of automation**, extrapolating the trend of computing: more and more
  autonomous entities doing digital work, then physical work some time later. [1:17:36](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=4656) [1:18:12](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=4692)
- It will still feel "extremely foreign", because it runs on clusters and much faster. [1:18:42](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=4722)
- **Most likely outcome in his view: a gradual loss of control and understanding.** Things
  get layered everywhere and fewer and fewer people understand them. [1:19:12](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=4752)
- Dwarkesh: understanding and control differ (corporate boards, the US president).
  Karpathy expects to lose both. His "sci-fi" version has no single entity taking over. Instead
  **multiple competing entities** become gradually more autonomous, some go rogue and others fight them,
  "this hot pot of completely autonomous activity that we've delegated to". [1:20:00](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=4800) [1:20:30](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=4830)
- **Loss of control comes from competition, not superior intelligence.** Even when entities
  act for individuals, society loses control over outcomes. [1:20:30](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=4830) [1:21:01](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=4861)

## The intelligence explosion is already happening

- Asked whether a million copies of him would cause an intelligence explosion: yes, **"but it's
  business as usual"**. We've been in an intelligence explosion for decades. GDP is an
  exponentially weighted sum of gradual automation: the industrial revolution, then compilers as
  early software automation. "We've been recursively self-improving and exploding for a
  long time." [1:21:31](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=4891) [1:22:01](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=4921)
- Earth from space looks boring, and then this "firecracker event" happens, which we're seeing in slow
  motion. [1:22:33](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=4953)

## You can't find technologies in GDP

- **He tried to find AI in GDP and convinced himself that's the wrong expectation.** You can't find
  computers or mobile phones in GDP either. The early iPhone ("2008, was it") had no App Store. Everything diffuses
  slowly and averages into **the same exponential**. AI is a new kind of computing
  and will diffuse the same way, though that exponential eventually gets "extremely vertical". [1:23:03](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=4983) [1:23:33](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=5013) [1:24:04](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=5044)
- Dwarkesh frames the alternatives: long-run growth went from ~0% to 0.02% (10,000 years ago)
  to 2%. Does AI take us to 20% or 200%, or does it keep us at the 2% that steam, railways,
  electrification and computers each sustained? [1:24:04](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=5044) [1:24:34](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=5074)
- **Karpathy: "my expectation is that it stays the same pattern."** Recursive
  self-improvement is business as usual. LLMs help engineers build the next LLM, just like
  Google Search, IDEs, autocomplete and Claude Code. [1:25:04](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=5104) [1:25:35](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=5135)

## Dwarkesh's counter-case, and Karpathy's reply

- Dwarkesh: true AGI is **labor itself**. The world is labor-constrained, and billions of extra
  smart minds that integrate themselves like skilled immigrants would be qualitatively different.
  Hong Kong and Shenzhen saw 10%+ growth for decades. Growth regimes have shifted before
  (0.2% → 2%). [1:26:05](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=5165) [1:26:36](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=5196) [1:28:40](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=5320) [1:29:10](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=5350)
- Karpathy: "pretty willing to be convinced", but **computing was already labor**
  (jobs disappeared to computers, and self-driving is computers doing labor). People
  imagine **"God in a box"**, but AI will do some things, fail at others, and be put
  into society gradually. [1:26:36](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=5196) [1:27:06](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=5226) [1:27:38](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=5258) [1:28:09](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=5289)
- "You're presupposing some discrete jump... that has basically no historical precedent
  that I can't find in any of the statistics." On the industrial revolution as a precedent he's "a little
  bit suspicious" of the pre-industrial data. [1:29:40](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=5380) [1:30:00](https://api.substack.com/feed/podcast/176425744/3127b612a1604474d2b28ab4e9b658f4.mp3#t=5400)
