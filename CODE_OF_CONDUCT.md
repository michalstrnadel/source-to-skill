# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

The full text — standards, enforcement responsibilities, scope, and
enforcement guidelines — is at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/> and
applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
other channel where you represent the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported privately to the maintainer:

- through a private report at
  <https://github.com/michalstrnadel/source-to-skill/security/advisories/new>
  (mark it as a Code of Conduct report), or
- via the contact options on the maintainer's GitHub profile,
  [@michalstrnadel](https://github.com/michalstrnadel).

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the
privacy and security of the reporter will be respected. The maintainer will
follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines (correction, warning,
temporary ban, permanent ban) in determining consequences.
